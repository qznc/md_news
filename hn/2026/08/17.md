## Technical Implementation

Anthropic's approach uses [steganography](https://declaude.org/watermarking/) rather than invisible characters. At each token generation point, words are dynamically sorted into "green" and "red" lists based on secret keys. Models become slightly more likely to choose from the green list, creating statistical fingerprints detectable only by Anthropic. This method, also used by [Google's SynthID](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/), adjusts probability scores without adding visible artifacts.
