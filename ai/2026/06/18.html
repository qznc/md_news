Long Contexts as Hidden Triggers: How Coherent Narratives Undermine AI Safety Guardrails

Large language models (LLMs) are often safeguarded using alignment techniques like reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), designed to prevent harmful outputs. The model’s statistical reasoning recalibrates to the dominant semantic structure of the input, making it vulnerable to unintended behavior shifts that occur deep within its architecture—long before output filters might intervene.
